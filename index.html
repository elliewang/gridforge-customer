Frequently asked questions

How do I understand my usage?

Review the Energy usage tab for a month-by-month breakdown. This demonstration uses synthetic values and does not connect to a meter.

Can I pay my bill here?

No. This static demo shows example billing records only. It does not process payments or connect to financial services.

How are service tickets handled?

New tickets are stored locally on your device. They can be viewed and exported but are not delivered to a live support team.

Is this an official Constellation site?

No. GridForge is an independent demonstration concept, not an official Constellation application.

What the assistant can do

Ask about usage, bills, local requests, energy efficiency, portal capabilities, and support. The assistant recognizes common intents and provides rule-based explanations using this demo's data.

✦ Works offline, without a paid AI API or secret credentials. Not a generative AI model.
